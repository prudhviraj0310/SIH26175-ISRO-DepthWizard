What DepthWizard Does — And What It Doesn’t

DepthWizard turns single-frame optical satellite imagery into an interactive 3D terrain reconstruction. The vertical baseline is anchored to authoritative digital elevation models (DEMs). Depth model outputs (Depth Anything V2) are presented as relative depth gradients or fusion supplements — never masqueraded as certified metric heights.

Operational Boundaries & Rigorous Caveats

Certification Declaration

Monocular depth estimation from single optical frames is fundamentally subject to scale-depth ambiguity and illumination biases. Without ground control points (GCPs) and geodetic stereo tie-points, all outputs are designated as research prototypes and triage screens. DO NOT deploy for certified engineering clearances, flight safety, or critical flood barriers without surveyed ground confirmation.
