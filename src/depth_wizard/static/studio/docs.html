How the three pages work

01
DW Studio

Pick a scene from the library, search Sentinel-2 by place and date on the map, or drop your own GeoTIFF/PNG/JPG. The tier badge tells you up front whether you get metric height (≤ 2.4 m/px), DEM terrain, or a relative preview. Each start becomes a job you can pin, save or reopen.

02
3D Explorer

Orbit, fly through and measure the reconstructed surface. Switch hypsometric, optical, slope, relative-depth and DEM layers, flood the terrain with a water level, draw transects, undo/redo every change, and take screenshots.

03
Research & Docs

This page: the calibration maths, validation numbers, where every dataset comes from, and an honest list of what is and isn't built yet.

Traditional · stereo photogrammetry
  • Requires two+ co-registered views with base-to-height ratio 0.3–0.8
  • Tasking cost and revisit delay; fails on occlusion & textureless roofs
  • Dense matching (SGM) yields noisy façades and voids
DepthWizard · monocular + anchors
  • Any single archival frame — Maxar VHR, Sentinel-2, or synthetic
  • Depth Anything v2 supplies dense, sharp relative structure
  • SRTM / GLO-30 / FABDEM anchors pin scale and datum to metres

Mathematical pipeline

01
Relative depth
d̂(x) = f_θ(I) , f_θ = Depth Anything v2 (ViT-L, fine-tuned)
02
Affine calibration
Ĥ(x) = s · d̂(x) + t
03
Huber-loss fit on anchors A
(s*, t*) = argmin Σ_{a∈A} ρ_δ( s·d̂(a) + t − H_DEM(a) ) , ρ_δ(r) = ½r² if |r|≤δ, else δ(|r| − ½δ)
04
Tier promotion
Tier 1 = DEM baseline → Tier 2 = calibrated metric DSM when RMSE_anchor < τ
